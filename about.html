How We Started

The Story of Our Studio

FermentStudio was born out of a passion for slow food, sustainable living, and the incredible science behind naturally fermented products. What started as a small personal kitchen experiment in 2012 has now grown into a thriving community of thousands.

We realized that while fermentation is an ancient tradition, many people were intimidated by the process. We set out to demystify the art, making it approachable, safe, and incredibly delicious for everyone.
