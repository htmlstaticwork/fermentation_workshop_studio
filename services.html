FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to bring anything?

No! We provide all jars, ingredients, aprons, and tools. Just bring your curiosity and appetite for learning.

Can children join?

Yes! Children aged 12+ are welcome with an adult accompanying them. It's a fantastic, hands-on science experience.

Is everything organic?

We prioritize locally sourced organic produce from our certified partner farms whenever possible — and for most workshops, everything is certified organic.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancellations made more than 48 hours before a session receive a full refund or session credit. Contact us and we'll find the best solution for you.
